# When to post

Suggest a strong slot when the user asks when to post, or gives a date without
a time. A request to post with no date publishes now; add no suggestion. Call
it a strong slot, never the best time: it comes from published research on
when audiences engage, not from the user's own data. For a date without a
time, put the slot in the confirmation with a few words on why instead of
asking for the time.

Strong slots, in the audience's local time:

- YouTube: Sunday or Tuesday, 9 to 11 AM.
- YouTube Shorts: Friday, 4 to 7 PM; other days 7 to 9 PM.
- TikTok: Tuesday to Thursday, 2 to 6 PM.
- Instagram: Tuesday to Thursday, 1 to 2 PM.
- Facebook: Tuesday or Wednesday, noon to 3 PM.
- LinkedIn: Tuesday to Thursday, 1 to 4 PM; never weekends.
- X: Tuesday to Thursday, noon to 3 PM.

Pick one time on the hour inside the window. Keep a date the user gave even
when it is not a strong day. Posts to several platforms at once get a time
inside every window; when the windows don't overlap, suggest a time per
platform. If the window has already passed on the date given, keep the date,
suggest a later time that day and say it is outside the strong window.

The audience's location comes from what the user said, otherwise from their
time zone; say the slot is based on it and that they can tell you if most of
their audience is elsewhere. With neither, ask once where most of their
audience is. A country with several time zones uses its most populous one
(Eastern for the US and Canada). The slot is a time in the audience's zone:
carry that zone into scheduling instead of re-reading the hour in the user's
zone, and show both, e.g. "Tuesday at 10 AM your time (1 PM Eastern)". If that lands on
another day for the user than the one they asked for, say so and ask which
day to keep.
